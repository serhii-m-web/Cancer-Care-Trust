Make a Donation

Without the generous support of people like you, we would not be able to continue supporting people living with cancer. Every contribution, whatever its size, helps us reach and support more people.

Fundraise for Cancer Care Trust

You could host a bake sale, take part in a challenge event, run a marathon or create your own fundraising idea. Whether you have a few hours or want to take on something bigger, we would love to hear from you.

Donate Your Clothes

Cancer Care Trust works with Clothes Relief, which collects good-quality donated clothing, accessories, shoes and linen on our behalf. This partnership makes it easy to donate unwanted items from home while supporting people affected by cancer across the UK.

Leave a Gift in Your Will

A gift in your will can help Cancer Care Trust continue supporting people living with cancer in the future. If you choose to remember Cancer Care Trust in your will, our full registered charity details should be included. We recommend speaking to a solicitor when making or changing a will.
