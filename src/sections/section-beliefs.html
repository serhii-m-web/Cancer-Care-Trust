Our Beliefs

Support Should Consider the Whole Person

Cancer can affect emotional and psychological wellbeing as well as physical health.

Cancer Care Trust believes that emotional and psychological support is an important part of supporting people living with cancer and maintaining quality of life.

This is why our approach recognises both the emotional and practical pressures that can accompany cancer.

By directing support towards people and organisations that may benefit from additional financial help, we aim to make difficult circumstances a little easier to manage.
