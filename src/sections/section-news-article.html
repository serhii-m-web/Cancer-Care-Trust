A cancer diagnosis can change daily life almost overnight. Alongside medical appointments and treatment decisions, many people face new financial pressures, practical challenges at home, and uncertainty about where to turn for help.

How practical support helps

Cancer Care Trust works to ease some of those pressures by providing practical and financial support to people affected by cancer, and by funding organisations that improve cancer care, support and research across the UK.

For families, even small forms of support can make a meaningful difference — help with unexpected costs, guidance on available services, or simply knowing there is somewhere to ask for advice without judgement. You can also get in touch with our team if you are unsure where to start.

What support can include

  • Help with unexpected everyday costs during treatment
  • Guidance on local and national support services
  • Signposting to specialist organisations and advice lines
  • Practical tools that reduce stress at home and at work

Steps many people take first

  1. Speak with someone who understands cancer-related pressures
  2. Review what practical help is already available locally
  3. Ask about financial support options that may apply to you
  4. Share needs with carers, family or trusted friends
We believe support should consider the whole person. Cancer is only one part of someone’s life, and the right information and practical tools can help people manage its impact across emotional, financial and everyday circumstances.

Looking ahead together

If you or someone you care about needs support, or if you would like to help others receive it, our team is here to listen and guide you towards the next step. Read more on our News page or explore How We Help.

From local bake sales to challenge events, supporters across the country help Cancer Care Trust continue this work every day.
