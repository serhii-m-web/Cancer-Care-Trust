Why We Are Here

A cancer diagnosis can bring pressures that extend far beyond treatment itself. For people living with cancer and those supporting them, everyday costs, travel, household bills and other practical needs can become more difficult to manage. Cancer Care Trust provides financial support to help ease some of these pressures during difficult times. Our grants can contribute towards practical needs including transport, bills and everyday expenses

Our support is not limited to the person diagnosed. We also recognise the pressures experienced by carers, friends and family members supporting someone living with cancer.
