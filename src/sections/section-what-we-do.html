What We Do

Our work focuses on two connected areas: supporting people affected by cancer directly and helping other charities continue their important work.

Carers & Families

Supporting those who care

Cancer affects more than one person. We support carers and families facing practical, emotional and financial pressures.

Financial Support

Helping ease the financial pressure

We provide financial support to people affected by cancer, helping with everyday costs during and after treatment.

Practical Support

Support for everyday challenges

From transport and household bills to essential daily needs, we help make life a little easier when it matters most.

Supporting Cancer Charities

Helping vital work continue

We provide funding to cancer charities, helping them deliver essential services and support progress in cancer research.
