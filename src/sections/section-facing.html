What Are You Facing Right Now?

Cancer can affect much more than physical health. Emotional wellbeing, confidence, relationships, finances and day-to-day life can all be impacted.

Cancer Care Trust understands that people may need more than medical treatment alone to cope with these wider challenges.

Need Someone to Talk To

A cancer diagnosis can bring fear, uncertainty and difficult questions. Having someone to talk to can make things feel more manageable.

{{{picture "/images/facing-img-2.png" "Person feeling anxious or overwhelmed by cancer" class="support__slide-image"}}}

Feeling Anxious or Overwhelmed

Cancer can create strong emotional pressure. Support can help you cope with anxiety, treatment and the challenges of everyday life.

{{{picture "/images/facing-img-3.png" "Person feeling alone or unsupported after a cancer diagnosis" class="support__slide-image"}}}

Feeling Alone or Unsupported

Cancer can feel isolating, even when people are around you. The right support can help you feel understood and less alone.
